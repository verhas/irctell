A little more than chat

Everything in its
right place.

Irctell keeps the familiar rhythm of IRC and adds the details that make a long-running conversation easier to follow.

◉

Networks, together

Keep multiple networks in a single window. Switch between channels and private chats without juggling a stack of windows.

⌕

History that helps

Search conversations, revisit private chats, and keep track of mentions and nicks in a local log.

⌘

Your own automation

Connect scripts to IRC events using simple text or structured JSON Lines, with controls for what each script can do.

♫

Listen your way

Have conversations read aloud. Choose system voices or download optional natural voices, then tune the voice for each nick.

✦

Replies, on your Mac

Optional Apple Intelligence suggestions stay on-device. They're yours to review, edit, and send when you're ready.

⌁

Flexible connections

Use TLS and SASL, or route a connection through Tor, SOCKS, or SSH to fit your setup.

Your conversations stay yours

Local by
default.

Your settings and message history live on your Mac. Irctell connects directly to the IRC networks you choose.
